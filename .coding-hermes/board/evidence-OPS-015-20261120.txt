WHY (source-of-truth, systemd v259 src/core/service.c — fetched 2026-11-20 from
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/systemd/systemd/v259/src/core/service.c):
  service.c:2118-2119  `if (s->result == SERVICE_SUCCESS) s->result = f;`
      service_enter_dead() only adopts a new result when the previous result was
      SUCCESS. A prior exit-code failure therefore can never be overwritten by the
      start-limit result.
  service.c:5673-5690  service_test_startable() is the ONLY writer of
      SERVICE_FAILURE_START_LIMIT_HIT: `service_enter_dead(s, SERVICE_FAILURE_START_LIMIT_HIT, false)`.
  service.c:5884       [SERVICE_FAILURE_START_LIMIT_HIT] = "start-limit-hit"
  unit.c:1848-1859     unit_test_start_limit(): "Start request repeated too quickly."
Consequence: on this manager Result=start-limit-hit is observable only when the last
death was a SUCCESS (clean exit under Restart=always). For a genuinely crashing unit
the brake is observable as the refusal itself (journal "Start request repeated too
quickly.", restarts capped at StartLimitBurst, ActiveState=failed, is-failed rc=0).
Both arms are recorded below.
